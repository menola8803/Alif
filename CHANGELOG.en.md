# Changelog (English)

The app shows the section for a new version in the language chosen in Settings. Keep this file in step with CHANGELOG.md.

## 0.5.4
- Sound at prayer: choose the phone’s sound, silent, one of five adhans (Makkah, Mishary al-ʿAfāsī, ʿAbd al-Bāsiṭ, al-Minshāwī, Nāṣir al-Qaṭāmī) with a preview, the Fajr adhan at Fajr, an alarm/ringtone from the phone or an own sound file; can play as an alarm.
- Night clock: a clock and the prayer times on the whole screen, kept on and dimmed – works on every phone. Screen saver: help about choosing "While charging" and a preview picture.

## 0.5.3
- Qur’an: choose Surahs or Juz. The juz list shows the 30 parts with name, start, end and number of sajdas; a juz is read straight through across the sūras. A gold line in a sūra shows where a new juz begins.
- Sajda: the sign ۩ in gold and "Sajda – prostrate here" at the 15 sajda verses, in both sūras and juz.

## 0.5.2
- Widget: gold design with a thin gold border, follows the light/dark theme; choose Everything or Prayer times only under Settings › Widget and screen saver. Can be put on the lock screen where the phone supports it.
- Screen saver with a clock and the prayer times (toned down in the light theme).
- All option rows have the same see-through look as the menu at the bottom.
- Today: one gold card for the Hijri calendar (date, next special day, sacred month); Mosques next to Qibla.
- The Qur’an translation is chosen only in Settings (the line in the sura panel is gone).
- Mosque map: the dot button zooms in where you are; add mosques by holding a finger on the map, remove mosques; the Aisha mosque and more Stockholm mosques added; mosques whose street is not found are included at an approximate place.
- Daily Awrad: hold a counter to reset it.

## 0.5.1
- **Mosque map** (Today › Mosques): an OpenStreetMap map as in Trace with all the mosques in Sweden (OpenStreetMap, Wikidata and islam.se), name, address, distance, directions and a list by distance.
- **Listen** under Translate: spoken Arabic becomes text in a beige box, transliteration and translation. The microphone is off by default, only open while you listen and closed when you leave the page; "On the phone only" is the default so no audio is sent to Google.
- **Home screen widget** with the next prayer, the Hijri date and today’s times.
- **Dhikr with the volume buttons** (turned on on the Dhikr page).

## 0.5.0
- **Recitation** in every sūra: a player at the bottom, verse by verse, the verse being recited is highlighted and followed. Default Maḥmūd Khalīl al-Ḥuṣarī; more reciters in Settings › Recitation.
- **Download sūras** with the ⤓ arrow in the sūra list or the player – then they play offline. Wifi only by default, and downloaded audio can be removed.
- **Tajwīd colours** with a legend, **word by word** (word meanings in English) and **ḥifẓ mode** (the text is hidden, each verse repeated 1–10 times).
- **Repetition cards** for the letters in Learn.
- **Backup** of settings, bookmarks, history and progress in a file.

## 0.4.3
- Learn to read: the letter’s four forms are shown two by two, with a number in a ring (1 alone, 2 at the start, 3 in the middle, 4 at the end) so the order is clear.

## 0.4.2
- Learn to read: the letter’s four forms are shown in four separate white boxes with round corners and space between them, and the letter fills the box better.
- Pictures in Learn and in Translate › Picture get the right size on the phone and no longer spill out of their box.

## 0.4.1
- Learn to read: the letter’s forms are shown whole in four boxes (alone, at the start, in the middle, at the end) with headings in the chosen language, instead of a table that was cut off. The pictures of where a letter is pronounced are cropped to their frame and sharper.
- The note that a letter does not join is shown only once.
- Today: the picture shows the Kaʿba, the dome or the door instead of just the middle of the photo.

## 0.4.0
- **New menu with five tabs:** Today, Qur’an, Translate, Learn and Dhikr, with new icons (Translate is an old dip pen with an Arabic stroke). The menu floats slightly see-through over the page. Settings open with the gear in the top right corner, which glows when a new version is out. History is under Settings.
- **Today:** a prayer clock (24 hours, the sun moves round and the next prayer glows with a countdown), today’s times with a notification per prayer, Daily Awrad, Qibla, the Hijri month and the next special day. At the bottom a picture of Mecca, Medina or Hagia Sophia with Allāhumma ṣalli ʿalā sayyidinā Muḥammad in gold – a new picture every time you tap Today.
- **Prayer times** from Aladhan with Muslim World League, default Stockholm, ʿaṣr Ḥanafī (Standard optional) and a rule for short summer nights. Pick a Swedish town, look up a place anywhere or use your location (off by default). Saved for the whole month and calculated on the phone without internet. Notifications at the five prayers.
- **Hijri calendar** with the Islamic month names in Arabic, transliteration and English/Swedish, the sacred months, special days and a ±1 day adjustment.
- **Qibla compass** with direction, distance and a buzz when you face it.
- **Qur’an:** the Swedish and English translations are built in, so the whole Qur’an works offline. Continue reading and bookmarks.
- **Translate:** Write, Picture (open png/jpeg/bmp and draw round the text), Camera and File (.docx, .odt, .pdf, pictures and text – mark it in the white box and translate).
- **Learn to read** following the book Iqraʾa!: all letters with the book’s pictures of where they are pronounced and their forms, Listen, vowel signs and questions switched on in the ⋮ menu (off by default). The whole book opens as a PDF.
- **Dhikr:** counter with vibration and goals, Daily Awrad with the Ihdāʾ and ṣalawāt.
- **Manual** in Swedish and English under Settings, and a privacy page.
- The release notes are shown in the language chosen in Settings.

## 0.3.3
- **Ihdāʾ (dedication)** in the Daily Awrad with the whole text: Arabic with vowel marks, tajwīd transliteration and its meaning in Swedish or English. You choose the living shaykh named in the last line (none, Shaykh Hishām Kabbānī or Shaykh ʿĀdil ar-Rabbānī).
- Transliteration keeps ﷺ and writes tāʾ marbūṭa without a vowel as -h.

## 0.3.2
- Camera on the phone: the photo is cut to the frame and made black and white before it is read.
- What was read is checked against the Qurʾān's vocabulary. Noise is not translated; you get choices instead: read with Claude, share to Google Lens, type the text or take a new photo.
- The read text is shown and can be corrected before it is translated again.

## 0.3.1
- Settings → Translation explains what each choice means: quality, cost, whether the text leaves the phone and where it goes.

## 0.3.0
- Several translation engines: Automatic, Claude, on the phone (Google ML Kit, free and offline) and MyMemory (free, online).
- Real-time translation while typing with the free engines; camera without a key (Tesseract).
- Qurʾān verses are recognised offline with any engine.
